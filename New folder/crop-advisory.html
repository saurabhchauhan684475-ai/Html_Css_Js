Crop Advisory

Practical reminders for common crops. This demo is educational, not a substitute for local agricultural advice.

🌾 Wheat · गेहूं

Monitor irrigation, weeds and signs of rust or other disease. Avoid unnecessary irrigation before heavy rain.

🌾 Paddy · धान

Watch water level, pests and nutrient symptoms. Follow locally recommended transplanting and fertilizer practices.

🌻 Mustard · सरसों

Check for aphids and fungal symptoms. Keep the field free from excess standing water.

🥔 Potato · आलू

Monitor late blight symptoms and soil moisture. Store harvested potatoes in a cool, ventilated place.

When something looks wrong

Take clear photos of the affected leaves/crop, note the date and field conditions, and contact a local agriculture expert or KVK before using a pesticide.
