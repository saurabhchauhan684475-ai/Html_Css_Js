Help Center

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us if something isn't working.

I didn't get my OTP. What do I do?

Wait 60 seconds and request a new code. Make sure your phone has signal and that you entered the number without any spaces.

Can I cancel or change a booked slot?

Yes — open "Track a Request" and use the cancel option there, then raise a new request for a different time.

What happens if I arrive late for my slot?

The centre will try to fit you in, but farmers who booked later slots get priority. Arriving on time keeps the queue fair for everyone.

Are the market prices official?

Prices shown are reference rates for planning — always confirm the final rate at the procurement centre itself.
