Farmer Document Checklist

Keep the documents commonly needed for procurement, banking and scheme applications ready. Exact requirements vary by service.

🪪 Identity

  • Aadhaar / valid ID
  • Mobile number linked to your services

🌱 Land & Farm

  • Land record / ownership document where required
  • Crop / sowing details

🏦 Banking

  • Bank account details / passbook
  • IFSC details when required

🎫 QueueSetu

  • Token slip / booking details
  • Procurement centre and slot time

Security reminder

Never upload or send sensitive documents to an unknown person. QueueSetu should never ask you for your ATM PIN, UPI PIN or OTP after login.
